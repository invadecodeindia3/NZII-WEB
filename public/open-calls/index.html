    About the Net Zero India Initiative

    The Net Zero India Initiative (NZII) is led by IIT Delhi’s School of Public Policy and supported by Prayas (Energy Group) and Princeton University’s Andlinger Center for Energy and Environment. Our work starts from one question:

    What will it take for India to reach net zero?

    We examine spatially resolved pathways to net-zero emissions by 2070 and their implications for employment, health, energy access, equity, capital allocation, land, water and biodiversity across India. All modelling tools and data used in the study are open access. Through the open calls, we aim to address emergent capacity deficits across the themes of the project.

    Research Frontier Networks by Schmidt Sciences

    The Schmidt Sciences Climate Center is funding Research Frontier Networks (RFNs), designed to cultivate regional talent and develop context-specific tools (e.g., regional databases, energy models, dashboards) that advance earth system research and inform energy and carbon transition pathways. Each RFN serves as an interdisciplinary, cross-sector research hub where academic institutes, research scientists, industry experts, and government representatives collaborate to address global climate challenges with solutions tailored to local needs. The RFN model targets national and sub-national scale research in the Global South, beginning in India with IIT Delhi as one hub, and expanding to other countries over time.

    Research Frontier Networks (RFNs) seek to build indigenous expertise and support the development of data and models that reflect the specific economic, social, and resource contexts of the Global South – contexts that are distinct from those of developed economies where most currently existing tools and models are developed.

      How we handle the information you give us when you apply for an open call.

      Who we are

      The Net Zero India Initiative at the School of Public Policy, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, Hauz Khas, New Delhi 110016. We decide how your information is used for these open calls.

      What we collect

      • Your name, contact details, designation and organisation.
      • Your answers, research plan and CV.

      Why we use it

      • To assess your application and tell you the outcome.
      • To arrange your visit, honorarium and travel if you are selected.

      We use your information only with your consent, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

      Who sees it

      The NZII team at IIT Delhi and the members of the selection committee. If you are selected, we share your name, project title and general-audience description with Schmidt Sciences and may publish them on our website. We do not sell your information.

      [Retention period to be confirmed by IIT Delhi.] You can ask us to delete your information at any time before a decision is made.

      You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent. Write to Ashwini Mudgal at ashwinim.cstaff@iitd.ac.in.
